// 06 · Protocol

From problem to product.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Before writing code, map the problem: who it's for, what data it needs, and what "done" looks like.

  2. 02

    Design

    Sketch the architecture, choose the stack, design the data model. This is where most bugs are prevented.

  3. 03

    Build & test

    Build in small increments, write tests alongside the code, refactor when something starts to feel heavy.

  4. 04

    Ship & learn

    Deployment isn't the end. Monitor, collect feedback, iterate. Software is never truly finished.
